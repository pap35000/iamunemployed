Privacy

The short version: we collect as little as possible, we only use your email to tell you when iAmUnemployed launches, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

iAmUnemployed is run by [YOUR NAME], [CITY], Greece. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address
  • if you choose to tell us: when your job search started, where you heard about us, and your country

The date you pick for the ticket on our homepage stays in your browser and is not sent to us. If you then open the waitlist form, we may fill that date in for you. It only reaches us if you submit the form.

Why we use it

To send you one email when iAmUnemployed launches, and to understand how many people are interested and where they found us. We use your data because you agreed to it when you signed up (consent, article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). We never sell it and never use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

The waitlist form runs on Google Forms and the answers are stored in Google Sheets, so Google processes them on our behalf. To send the launch email we may use an email service such as Brevo or Mailchimp.

How long we keep it

Until we have sent the launch email. If iAmUnemployed has not launched within 12 months, we delete the waitlist. You can ask us to delete your details sooner, at any time.

Other services this site uses

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Your browser fetches them from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Our host, [Cloudflare or Netlify], keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. [delete this line if you don’t turn analytics on]

Cookies

We don’t use cookies or advertising trackers. Your browser remembers the start date you picked so your ticket looks the same next time. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we’ll handle it within 30 days. If you think we have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at dpa.gr, or to the data protection authority where you live.

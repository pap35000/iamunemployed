# Feature log

One new member-visible feature ships at a time. Newest first.

## Shipped

- 2026-11-27: Clocked In badge. Members can earn a new Clocked In badge for a 30-day streak of check-ins or moves, follow its progress on the Me tab, and see how many days in a row are left in the Streaks card.
- 2026-11-26: Undo a move. Members who mis-tap a move on the Queue tab can open Undo a move and remove one of today's moves at a time, with their weekly goal, totals and Grind of the Week count following and their streak kept.
- 2026-11-25: Feed and move shortcuts. Members on desktop can press J and K to step through Feed posts, with focus landing on each post so Tab reaches its reactions, and M to jump straight to logging a move on the Queue tab.
- 2026-11-24: New support since your last visit. Members who come back to the app get a gentle toast listing the hugs, sames and other reactions other members left on their posts since they last looked, with a matching "+N new" note on the Me tab's Support received card.
- 2026-11-23: Last week in review. Members see a private recap card on the Queue tab with last week's applications, interviews, check-in days, most common mood, busiest day and how it compares with their weekly goal, and can hide it until next Monday.
- 2026-11-22: New best streak. Members on a run level with their old record see a nudge on the Queue tab that today's check-in or move beats it, and get a "new personal best" note in the toast (or milestone sheet) the day they break it.
- 2026-11-21: This week vs last week. Members can compare their applications, interviews, all moves and check-ins this week so far with last week on the Me tab, privately, with a fair note on how they are doing against the same day last week.
- 2026-11-20: Keyboard shortcuts. Members on desktop can switch tabs with 1 to 5, start a post with N and press ? for a shortcuts sheet, also reachable from a new row in the Me tab settings.
- 2026-11-19: Support received. Members can see on the Me tab how many hugs, sames and other reactions other members left on their own posts, anonymous ones included, and jump straight to their posts in the Feed.
- 2026-11-18: My posts in the Feed. Members can tap the new Mine chip in the Feed to see only their own posts, anonymous ones included, with a count and a guided empty state if they have not posted yet.
- 2026-11-17: Mood strip day details. Members can tap any day in their private 14-day mood strip on the Me tab to see that day's mood and the moves they logged, with a small dot marking days that had moves.
- 2026-11-16: Best streak ever. Members can see their current streak next to their best streak ever on the Me tab, with the day they reached it and how many days in a row it takes to beat it.
- 2026-11-15: Streak milestone celebration. Members get a celebration sheet when their check-in streak reaches 7, 14, 30, 60, 100, 200 or 365 days, and the Queue tab shows how many days are left to the next milestone.
- 2026-11-14: Days since last interview. Members can see on the Queue tab how many days have passed since they last logged an interview, with the date and a gentle nudge.
- 2026-11-13: Mood history strip. Members can see their own check-in moods for the last 14 days on the Me tab, privately, with a count of how many days they checked in.
- 2026-11-12: Weekly application goal. Members can set how many applications they want to send this week on the Queue tab, watch a progress bar fill as they log applications, and get a toast when they reach it.
